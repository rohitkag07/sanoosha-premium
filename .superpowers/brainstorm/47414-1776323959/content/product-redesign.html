The Sacred Significance

The 1 Mukhi Rudraksha, also known as Ek Mukhi, is governed by the Sun and represents Lord Shiva in his most supreme and undivided form. In Vedic tradition, it is considered the most powerful of all Rudraksha beads, believed to grant the wearer liberation from the cycle of birth and death (Moksha).

The bead is formed naturally on the tree Elaeocarpus ganitrus and takes 15–20 years to mature. The single face (mukha) that lines the surface is what gives this bead its rarity and spiritual potency.
